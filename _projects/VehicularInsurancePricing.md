# Overview
This project was born from an assignment given in ACTL30004 Actuarial Statistics. It was open ended, and required us to determine a method for pricing pure premiums for a vehicular insurance portfolio, implement it in a simple-to-use app, and provide a report fit to be presented to executives/managers in the workplace.
 
This assignment was primarily concerned with GLM's but I decided to extend it by playing around with concepts I learned and were scattered throughout my course.

Most of the work was previously done on R, but was later ported to python.

## Data
Point level data was given, with the following features per individual:
- ID
- Age
- Gender
- Driving experience: How long have they been driving for
- Education
- Credit score
- Vehicle ownership; do they own the vehicle
- Marital status
- Number of children
- Annual Mileage
- Vehicle type
- Speeding violations
- Past accidents
- Outcome
- Amount claimed
